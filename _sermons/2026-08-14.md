First: Ready or not, here comes the fall semester!  For essentially my entire
adult life, I’ve faced the end of every August with mixed feelings.  Part of me
is always confused — another semester? already? — but it is also always an
exciting time of year.  I will get to meet new people, make new connections,
learn new things, and teach new things.  Even if it doesn’t always go according
to plan, I am living a life that I mostly chose, doing lots of things that I
love to do.  I hope you all feel the same.

Second: Not everything will always go as planned.  That’s part of life.  We’ll
deal with what comes, but in many cases, it is easier to deal with problems
when they first show up.  But I won’t know when a potential problem should be
on my radar unless you tell me!  Helping you all out is a big part of me job as
DGS, so don’t feel shy about sending me a note when something comes up.  And,
of course, you don’t need the excuse of a problem to come and talk to me.  My
door is frequently open, and the booking link on my home page is always there.
