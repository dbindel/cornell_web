I know this semester has been rough for some of you, whether because of
personal circumstances or because of [gestures at the world at large].  It
hasn’t been the easiest time for me, certainly.  But apart from the usual
advice — food, sleep, exercise, and social activity — and trying to accept the
things I cannot change, maybe one of the best ways I know to cope is this:

"The best thing for being sad .. is to learn something. That is the only thing
that never fails. ... That is the only thing which the mind can never exhaust,
never alienate, never be tortured by, never fear or distrust, and never dream
of regretting.”  -- T.H. White, _The Once and Future King_

I read _The Once and Future King_ when I was younger, but forgot this
particular line until earlier this week, when I was reminded courtesy 
[The Marginalian](https://www.themarginalian.org/2020/01/13/t-h-white-once-and-future-king-merlyn/) 
(yes, I’ve pointed to this one before).  But it’s really a lovely
expression of something that I have always deeply believed.  I chose my path
for a simple reason: I am here because I want to know things, and I want to
share what I know.  To the extent that I have a calling, that is what it is.
But besides being a calling, this is the way that I make my way through when
the world is difficult.

Given the choices you all have made to be here, I think you probably share some of this same feeling.
