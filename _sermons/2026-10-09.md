A short thought today, but one I have chewed over a lot recently.  I believe
strongly in giving grace to those around me when things are tough.  I struggle
sometimes in showing that grace to myself.  Sometimes that’s because I know
more about my own situation than I know about what others are going through;
but I think, too, that a piece of this is simply about humility.  None of us
has perfect information, perfect planning, perfect insight.  We all muddle
though as best we can, and we try to find what joy and value we may in the
process.  For me, part of giving myself grace is recognizing where my tendency
to self-judgement comes from trying to impose a standard that is maybe just a
little ridiculous.  We all fall down sometimes, we all need help sometimes, we
all come up short sometimes.  But if we are humble about our errors, at least
we might be able to learn from the process.
